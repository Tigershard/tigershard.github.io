Stripes

A personal Android app for organizing a vinyl record collection.

Stripes keeps a catalogue of the records you own and the ones you want. You can:

  • add records by hand, by searching Discogs, or by scanning the barcode on the sleeve
  • browse your collection as a list or a grid, sorted and filtered the way you like
  • keep a wishlist for the record shop
  • keep a copy of your collection in a Google Sheet in your own Google Drive

Google Sheets

If you connect a Google account, Stripes creates one spreadsheet, called "Vinyl Organizer", in your Drive and keeps it up to date with your collection. It asks only for Google's drive.file permission. That means it can see and change only the files it created itself, and nothing else in your Drive.

Stripes is a personal project. It isn't sold, has no ads, and has no server of its own: your records stay on your phone and in your own Google Drive.
